Shattering Mirror

A practice in rigid body dynamics and shattering FX

Project Breakdown

This was my first time creating a shattering/rigid body dynamics effect in Houdini. I focused a lot on finding good reference and matching the shapes and motion that I saw. I also practiced layering multiple effects to get the look that I wanted, using multiple particle simulations on top of the RBD simulation to create a more explosive shattering effect.

  • Responsible for shattering FX, particle FX, FX shading, lighting, and rendering
  • Mirror shattering FX created with Houdini Rigid Body Dynamics workflow
  • Particle shard FX created with Houdini POPS
  • FX shaded and lit in Houdini Solaris
  • Rendered with Houdini Karma
