Vintage Suitcase

Shading exercise of a vintage suitcase

Project Breakdown

I shaded this suitcase as an assignment for a shading class, focusing on learning how to use Adobe Substance 3D Painter and RenderMan in Houdini. I liked the challenge of shading multiple cohesive materials within the same object. I also liked learning more about RenderMan and how to set up the workflow between Substance Painter and Houdini.

  • Responsible for shading
  • Suitcase shaded in Adobe Substance 3D Painter
  • Shaders created in Houdini Solaris
  • Rendered with RenderMan
