Potted Plant Palooza!

A seemingly innocent potted plant comes to life....

Project Breakdown

An innocent looking potted plant comes to life... turning out to be a monster with a bite! The initial idea was to create some sort of ordinary object that turns into a monster, like a mimic in the game Dungeons and Dragons. I had a lot of fun coming up with the idea, design, and movement for the transformation from plant to monster for this character.This was my first rigging project, and it took some time to figure out all of the ins and outs of rigging, but I learned a lot!

  • Responsible for all aspects
  • Character and environment modeled, rigged, and animated in Maya
  • Textured in Substance Painter
  • Curtain cloth FX created in Maya
  • Lit and rendered in Arnold
