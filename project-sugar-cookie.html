Sugar Cookie

A mock product video for a sugar cookie company.

Project Breakdown

I love a good sugar cookie! This project was heavily inspired by the product photography and videos for Crumbl Cookies. I live in an area where Crumbl Cookies are heavily advertised, so I thought I'd try to make product video myself and have some fun with it! I love bright colors, so I wanted to incorporate limes and lime green into the project. I'm really happy with how my first dip into product videos turned out!

  • Responsible for all aspects. Sugar Cookie was modeled in Maya and textured in Substance Painter. FX created in Houdini, project rendered in Renderman.
  • FX created in Houdini, project lit in Houdini Solaris and rendered in Renderman.
