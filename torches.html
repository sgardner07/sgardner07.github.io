Torches

A practice in pyro FX

Project Breakdown

This was my first time creating a pyro effect Houdini. Similar to the shattering mirror project, I found a lot of reference and tried to match the shape and motion of my torch flames to it. I also layered a particle effect on top of the pyro sim to create embers. Overall, this project taught me a lot about the technicality behind Houdini Pyro effects!

  • Responsible for torch flame FX, ember FX, FX shading, lighting, and rendering.
  • Flame FX created with Houdini Pyro
  • Ember FX created with Houdini POPS
  • FX shaded and lit in Houdini Solaris
  • Rendered with Houdini Karma
