Rainy Forest FX

A dark and stormy forest scene with some fun FX!

Project Breakdown

This was a personal project to practice creating a realistic-feeling scene through layering different kinds of FX. I also used it as a way to to learn more about Houdini Vellum and its different uses. I worked on this project at the same time as I was doing shot work on a short film, and I feel like it helped me learn how to layer FX and leverage multiple simulations to get the look that I want.

  • Responsible for layout, tree motion, rain, water, grass, fog, FX shading, and lighting
  • Rain and water FX simulated with Houdini POPS and fluid surfacing
  • Tree motion and grass simulated with Houdini Vellum workflows
  • Fog simulated with Houdini Pyro
  • Lit in Houdini Solaris, rendered with Houdini Karma
